Dry rotisn't dry.

Dry rot and wet rot are the same problem caught at different times. Both need water. Here's what that means for the fix.

Dry rot is the most common thing we fix, and the name is wrong. Rot is a fungus eating wood, and it can't do that without water. The wood only looks dry because the fungus is done with it.

what rot actually is

Wood decay fungi feed on the parts of wood that make it strong. They need wood, air, a mild temperature and moisture. Take away the moisture and they stop. Wood that stays wet is food. Wood that dries out is just wood.

The US Forest Products Lab puts the line at about 20 percent moisture content. Below that, decay fungi can't get going. Around 30 percent, the wood fibers are saturated. Rot on a house happens somewhere above that 20 percent line, often for months at a time during the wet season.

so what's wet rot?

Dry rot and wet rot are the names people use for the same problem at different moments. Wet rot is what you find while the wood is still soaked: dark, soft, spongy. Dry rot is what's left after: brown, cracked into little cubes, crumbling when you touch it. Both started with water.

why the name matters

If you think dry rot happens to dry wood, you'll fix the wood and leave the water. We see it all the time. A soft board gets replaced and painted and looks great for a few years. Then the new board goes soft in the same spot, because the leak behind it never got fixed.

the leak is still there

By the time rot crumbles, the water has already done its job. It's usually still coming in. Look up from the rot: gutter, flashing, window head, roof edge.

quick check

A board crumbles into brown powder, but it feels dry today. What do you do?

what to do about it

  1. 01

    probe it

    Push a screwdriver into the wood. Sound wood stops it. Rot lets it sink in.

  2. 02

    find the water

    Look uphill from the soft spot. Rot is almost always below where the water gets in.

  3. 03

    cut it out

    Remove the rotted wood back to solid, dry wood on every side.

  4. 04

    stop the water

    Fix the flashing, gutter, joint or grading that made it wet. Then put new wood in.

replace the wood. stop the water.

Siding Repair in Bellevue, the short version.

Siding fails in the same few places: the bottom row near the ground, under windows, at butt joints, and where a roof edge stops against a wall. That last spot needs kickout flashing, and plenty of houses don't have it. Paint peeling at the bottom row is usually the first thing a homeowner notices.

Once water gets behind siding, it soaks the sheathing and studs. On a Lynnwood house, the owner found soft siding while watering plants. Behind it, the flashing had been missing since the house was built, and some studs had rotted. That's why we open the wall when the siding is soft. A new board over a soft stud fixes nothing.

If the damage is contained, we replace the bad boards and match what's there. If it's spread across the wall, a re-side can make more sense. We repair wood and fiber cement siding in Seattle, the Eastside and Snohomish County. We don't repair vinyl.

siding repair questions

The questionswe always get.

  • Can you repair siding without replacing the whole wall?

    Yes, when the damage is contained and the wall behind it is sound. We probe and open it to find out.

  • How do you check for rot behind the siding?

    We probe, take moisture readings, and open a section where the signs point to it. You see what we find.

  • What affects the cost and schedule?

    How much siding is involved, what's behind it, access, height, and matching the existing material and paint.

  • Will the repair be ready for paint?

    We agree on primer, paint and color matching in the estimate, so you know what's included.

  • Do you repair vinyl siding?

    No. Our siding repair covers wood and fiber cement.

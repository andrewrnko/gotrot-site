how we fix it

Find the water.Then fix the wood.

Same order on every Wood Frame Repair job. Nothing gets closed up until the wood behind it is dry and sound.

  1. 01

    open

    We remove siding or trim until we reach sound framing.

  2. 02

    support

    Temporary support goes in where the load needs it.

  3. 03

    replace

    Rotted studs, plates, beams and sheathing come out and new framing goes in.

  4. 04

    fix the water

    Flashing and the water path get fixed before the wall is closed.

  5. 05

    close

    Wrap, siding, trim and paint as agreed, and a walkthrough.

Wood Frame Repair, the short version.

Framing rot is the rot you find after the trim or siding comes off. Water gets in at a missing flashing or a failed joint and runs down inside the wall for years. By the time the outside looks bad, the studs and sheathing behind it can be soft. Doors and windows that start to stick nearby are often the first clue.

In Snohomish, a 1980 home had hidden rot in a load-bearing corner, from water getting past inadequate flashing. In Woodinville, poor flashing at a chimney rotted the siding, sheathing and framing next to it. Both looked like siding problems from outside. That's normal. Framing rot almost never announces itself.

We open the wall to good wood, support the load where needed, replace the rotted framing and sheathing, and fix the flashing before the wall gets closed. If anything points to a foundation problem, we tell you to bring in a structural engineer. We do frame repair in Seattle, on the Eastside and across Snohomish County.

frame repair questions

The questionswe always get.

  • How do I know if the rot is structural?

    You often can't from outside. If a soft spot is near a corner, a beam, a door or window opening, or the wall behind it gives, it needs to be opened and checked.

  • What if there are insects in the wood?

    Signs of termites or carpenter ants mean a pest specialist treats it first. Then we repair the wood and fix the water that drew them.

  • Does structural repair need a permit?

    Sometimes, depending on the city and the scope. We tell you during the estimate.

  • What if you find more once the wall is open?

    We stop, show you, and price it before doing more. Nothing extra happens without your okay.

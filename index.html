what gives rot away

how we find it

dry rotisn't dry.

Rot is a fungus, and it needs water to grow. So we don't stop at the soft spot. We follow the water back to where it gets in.

  1. 01

    poke it.

    A sound board stops a screwdriver. Rotted wood lets it sink in, and it comes back out wet or stringy.

  2. 02

    meter it.

    A moisture meter shows how wet the wood is. Above about 20 percent, the fungi that cause rot can start to grow.

  3. 03

    find the water.

    Then we trace it uphill. Flashing, gutters, a missing kickout, siding sitting on soil. Fix the rot but not the leak and it comes back.

questions

Asked everysingle week.

  • What is dry rot?

    Dry rot is wood decay caused by fungi. The name is misleading: the fungus needs wet wood to grow. The wood only looks dry once the rot is done with it.

  • How do I know if I have dry rot?

    Push a screwdriver into the wood. Sound wood stops it. Rot lets it sink in. Other signs are paint that keeps peeling in one spot, brown wood that cracks into cubes, and a musty smell.

  • Does dry rot spread?

    It spreads wherever the wood stays wet. Above about 20 percent moisture, decay fungi can grow into nearby wood. Fix the water and it stops.

  • How much does dry rot repair cost?

    It depends on how far the rot goes, whether framing is involved, access, and what it takes to fix the water source. We price it after we've looked, and estimates are free for eligible projects.

  • How long does a repair take?

    Most repairs take 1 to 3 days. Larger structural jobs take a week or more. You get the schedule with your estimate.

  • Do you fix the cause, or just the wood?

    Both. We find where the water gets in and fix it as part of the repair. If the source is the roof covering or plumbing, we tell you, because that's another trade.

  • Do you offer a warranty?

    Yes. All our work carries a 24-month warranty.

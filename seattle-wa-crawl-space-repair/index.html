Crawl Space Repair, the short version.

The crawl space is where nobody looks. It's dark, damp and full of wood: sill plates, joists, the underside of your subfloor. In a climate as wet as ours, a crawl space with a torn vapor barrier or water running toward the foundation can keep that wood wet for years. You usually smell it before you see it.

Find the water first. A new vapor barrier won't fix water being directed at the foundation, and replacing one rotted joist won't fix a plumbing leak above it. The repair has to match the source, or it's back in a few years. That's the order on every crawl space job.

We replace rotted joists, sill plates and subfloor, replace damaged vapor barriers, and deal with the drainage or leak behind it. We do crawl space work in Seattle, on the Eastside and in Snohomish County. Dry months are the easiest time to work under a house, but rot doesn't wait for summer.

crawl spaces questions

The questionswe always get.

  • What are the signs of crawl space rot?

    A musty smell, soft or bouncy floors, wet soil or standing water below, and dark or fuzzy wood on the joists and sills.

  • Will a new vapor barrier fix it?

    Only if ground moisture is the problem. If water is running toward the foundation or a pipe is leaking, that has to be fixed too.

  • Can rotted joists be fixed without lifting the house?

    Often, yes, with temporary support while the wood is replaced. House lifting and foundation work are outside our scope.

  • Does insurance cover crawl space damage?

    That depends on your policy and the cause. Your insurer decides coverage, not us. We can share photos of what we find.

  • When is the best time to fix it?

    Dry months are easiest, but we work year-round. Waiting usually means more wet wood.
